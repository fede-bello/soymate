Why soybean first

Soy is where we can build it today. Lentils are where the money is.

Sources: Mendeley datasets d97rjyk2tz and v6vzvfszj6; FranceAgriMer oilseed sheet 2026 (45 kt); Terres Inovia soy and lentil quality sheets 2025; Terres Univia price observatory (soy gap “très faible”), Terres Inovia 2024 (~70 €/t).

Why soybean if the problem is lentils? Because soy is the only bean with public photos of damaged seeds: about 8,000 of them. For lentils, we searched and found none. Soy is also a real French food crop: 45,000 tonnes a year go to tofu and soy drinks, and in 2025 one lot in three had too many unripe seeds. But food soy is worth only 20 to 70 euros more per tonne than feed. Lentils are worth about 550 euros more. So soy is where we build and test the method today, and lentils are where it pays. (~25 s)
