SoyMate

A seed grade from a phone photo,
that the farmer can check too.

[Team names]

At harvest, someone at the cooperative spreads a sample from each truck on a table and counts the damaged seeds by eye. That count sets what the farmer is paid. SoyMate does it from one phone photo, and says how sure it is. (~10 s)
