SoyMate

A seed grade from a phone photo,
that the farmer can check too.

[Team names]

Every harvest, someone at a grain cooperative takes a sample from a truck, spreads it on a table and counts damaged seeds by eye. That count decides what the farmer is paid. We built a tool that does it from a phone photo, and shows how sure it is. (~20 s)
