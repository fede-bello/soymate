Built on soybean, the only bean with public photos of damaged seeds. Model: frozen DINOv2 features + a simple classifier, runs on a laptop.

Here's what we built. The person at the cooperative takes a few photos of the sample with a phone. The app finds every seed, checks each one (good, broken, stained, unripe, or with a hole), and says how much of each there is and how sure it is. If it's not sure enough, it asks for more photos instead of guessing. We built it on soybean because it's the only bean with public photos of damaged seeds.
