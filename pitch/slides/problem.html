Bruchid: a small beetle. Its larva grows inside the seed and leaves a round hole when it comes out.

Source: Terres Univia / Terres Inovia, lentil seed quality survey, harvest 2025: mean 24.4 % damaged seeds per lot, 40 lots measured, range 1.0 to 75.5 %.

In 2025, French lentils had their worst year for a small beetle called the bruchid. Its larva grows inside the seed and leaves a hole. In an average truckload, about one seed in four was damaged, and it varied a lot: some trucks were almost clean, others had three seeds in four damaged. Farmers have no spray left that works against it in the field. (~20 s)
