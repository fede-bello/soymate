Is there money in this? In Australia, grain buyers already pay a company to grade lentils with AI. In France, nobody does it: the French leader only grades cereals like wheat and barley. Lentils sold to eat are worth about 550 euros more per tonne than animal feed, so getting the count right matters. And ours needs only a phone. Soy alone is a small market; lentils come next, then other European countries. (~25 s)
