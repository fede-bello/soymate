What we built

How much you can trust it

The public data leaked

84 % of its test photos came from the same original photos as its training set. Split by capture session, the score falls from 0.89 to 0.85.

Tested on real photos

Counting: off by 1.7 seeds per photo (1.1 %) on farmer photos. Packed lots: 94 % of seeds found; once seeds are located, % broken within 1.5 points on 6 lots it never saw.

Is it seeing the damage?

A test can tell which dataset a seed came from every time, so we score each dataset on its own. Holes are still found on the days with both classes (F1 0.97).

What a photo can’t see

a larva still inside a seed, before it makes its hole. Cutting pea seeds open found about 3 times more damage than looking.

How many seeds are enough

200 seeds can be off by up to 6 points; about 1,000 (a few photos) by less than 3. Single seeds are often wrong, so we always show the range.

Sources: our results at the event (results/README.md: R1, R2, R4a, R4b, dataset-ID check; Mendeley d97rjyk2tz, v6vzvfszj6, t94nzy4ptz, hwdtghttp7); LABOSEM 2022; binomial 95 % ranges at 25 % damage.

Can you trust it? First, the best public dataset leaked: most test photos came from the same originals as training, so scores looked better than they were. We fixed that and report the honest number. On real farmer photos it counts within about 2 seeds. On packed lots it never saw, it finds 94 % of the seeds, and once seeds are located the share of broken ones is within 1.5 points. And we say what we can't do: a model can tell which dataset a photo came from, so we never mix them in one score, and a photo can't see a larva inside a seed. (~30 s)
