What a photo can’t see

a larva still inside a seed, before it makes its hole. Cutting pea seeds open found about 3 times more damage than looking.

How many seeds are enough

200 seeds can be off by up to 6 points; about 1,000 (a few photos) by less than 3. When it’s unsure, the app asks for more.

Sources: our audit of Mendeley d97rjyk2tz (80-84 % of test images leaked; done before the event, declared); LABOSEM 2022 (pea ~4 % visual vs 11.8-13.5 % dissected); binomial 95 % ranges at 25 % damage.
