Evidence and limits

What we can and cannot conclude

Pea: one development image

40 labelled polygons matched; 8 extra background masks. Review is not independent validation.

Soybean: imperfect labels

A grouped validation review found missed and unmatched boxes. We have not measured natural multi-grain damage accuracy.

Only the visible surface

A photo cannot rule out hidden infestation. No automatic food-grade verdict is supported.

Sampling uncertainty is separate from detector mistakes and unrepresentative samples. More images alone do not fix systematic errors.

Sources: Zenodo pea demonstration subset; Shinde et al. soybean dataset v2; SoyMate grouped validation review; LABOSEM 2022.

These are exploratory checks. The pea image was used during development and contains no reference hole labels. Soybean validation annotations are imperfect, and we have not established damage accuracy on a multi-grain pea or lentil sample. A visible surface image also cannot reveal every hidden larva. (~25 s)
