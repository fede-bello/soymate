The damage stays the same. What changes is who knows, and when, and that decides who pays for the loss.

Here's what changes. The farmer can photograph a sample before the harvest leaves the farm and see what the cooperative will see. If it's badly damaged, they can have it cleaned first or choose a buyer who takes it as it is. If they disagree with the price cut, there's a photo to point to. The cooperative sends each truck straight to cleaning or to the food storage, and every site counts the same way. (~30 s)
