We don’t stop the beetle. We make the count fast, the same everywhere, and visible to both sides.

This is the key slide. Seeds get damaged, the damage sets the price, and today it's counted by eye, so it's slow, uneven, and nobody can check it. SoyMate doesn't stop the beetle: it gives the farmer and the cooperative the same count from a photo. The farmer knows the result before delivering, so they can clean the seeds first, choose who to sell to, or show a photo if they disagree. The cooperative knows right away where each truck goes, and every site counts the same way. Fewer arguments for both. (~30 s)
